Kick Clips: Terms of Service

Last updated 26 November 2026

Kick Clips is a personal, non-commercial tool made by Erik Karásek. It cuts the clips from his own Kick livestreams into videos and uploads them to his own YouTube channel and his own TikTok drafts. It is not offered to anyone else.

Use

  • Only content the operator made and owns is uploaded, and only to accounts the operator owns.
  • Every upload happens after a manual Release; TikTok uploads go to drafts and are published by hand in TikTok.
