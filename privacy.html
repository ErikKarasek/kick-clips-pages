Kick Clips: Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026, scopes revised the same day

Kick Clips is a personal tool made and run by Erik Karásek on his own computer. It turns the clips he makes during his own Kick livestreams into videos and, when he presses Release, uploads them to his own YouTube channel and sends them to the drafts of his own TikTok account. It has no other users, no website login and no server of its own.

What data it uses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to manage videos on the signed-in channel (the youtube scope): uploading the clips, setting and correcting their titles, descriptions and tags, and deleting a clip the operator removes. Nothing else is read from or changed on the account.
  • TikTok: the basic account info TikTok returns at sign-in (an account ID and display name, user.info.basic) and permission to send videos to that account's drafts (video.upload), permission to post a video the operator chose on the app's posting page with the caption, privacy and settings set there (video.publish), and a list of the account's public videos so a clip is never posted twice (video.list).
  • Kick: public stream, chat and clip data of the operator's own channel, read from Kick's public API.

Where it is kept

Sign-in tokens and videos are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the service they belong to (Google for YouTube uploads, TikTok for TikTok uploads). There are no analytics, ads or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google in Google Account permissions, for TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token files removes all stored data.

Third parties

YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok uploads are subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy. Kick Clips' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
